How I Work

Market & Product Approach

Fire-rated and high-security glass is a specified product, so the sale is won long before the order. My approach is proactive and specification-led, with every stakeholder involved at the right stage.

01
Specify Early
Engage architects at the design stage, so the right Vetrotech system is written into the specification from the start.
02
Align the Consultants
Work with MEP consultants and PMCs on fire and safety requirements, ratings and approvals for each application.
03
Support the Contractor
Guide contractors on product selection, submittals and site coordination, so what is built matches what was specified.
04
Deliver with Installers
Coordinate trained installation partners across Tamil Nadu, so the product performs as tested once it is on site.
